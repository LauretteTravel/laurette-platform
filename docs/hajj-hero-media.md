# Hajj season hero media

The main travel homepage now uses `public/media/hero/laurette-travel.mp4` and `laurette-travel-poster.webp`. It combines the supplied Gemini video and soundtrack, Laurette's existing logo, and Salim Da's [airplane footage](https://www.pexels.com/video/airplane-flying-above-serene-countryside-landscape-34671431/) under the [Pexels License](https://www.pexels.com/license/). The bundled video and poster can be replaced at these paths. Existing custom media settings and the video disable setting still apply.

- Makkah and the Kaaba footage by Ali Karim, [Famous Mosque](https://www.pexels.com/video/famous-mosque-5798349/). The fallback poster is a frame from this clip.
- Madinah and the Prophet’s Mosque footage by Md Amir Umar, [People at the Prophet’s Mosque in Madina, Saudi Arabia](https://www.pexels.com/video/people-at-the-prophet-s-mosque-in-madina-saudi-arabia-13348531/).
- Both clips are covered by the [Pexels License](https://www.pexels.com/license/). They were trimmed, faded, combined and encoded as a silent 720p MP4. No visual assets from the design references were redistributed.
